# Phase 2: three dimensions and their convergence

Generated by `python -m src.features.convergence`.

## Scores (higher = more ghost-like; the playlist group is never an input)

- **Identity**: share of 5 public-identity markers missing (verified, bio, links, gallery, Spotify 'This Is' playlist).
- **Production**: z(log10 ISRC-registrant house size, counted in distinct primary-artist names) + z(share of the artist's tracks whose credits name only the alias), averaged.
- **Demand**: z(-log10 followers per listener) + z(top 'Discovered on' playlist is one of the 50) + z(-share of listeners in the top city), averaged.

Source/(P)-line house size is left out of production on purpose: 42% of functional names list themselves as their own Source, which hides the house. Those self-labelled names sit in *larger* ISRC houses (median 264 names vs 98).

## Spearman correlation between dimensions (1,000 bootstrap resamples, 95% CI)

| subset          |    n | pair                  |   rho | ci            |
|:----------------|-----:|:----------------------|------:|:--------------|
| all artists     | 5024 | identity ~ production |  0.58 | [0.56, 0.60]  |
| all artists     | 5024 | identity ~ demand     |  0.57 | [0.55, 0.58]  |
| all artists     | 5024 | production ~ demand   |  0.48 | [0.46, 0.50]  |
| functional only | 4530 | identity ~ production |  0.49 | [0.46, 0.51]  |
| functional only | 4530 | identity ~ demand     |  0.44 | [0.41, 0.46]  |
| functional only | 4530 | production ~ demand   |  0.36 | [0.34, 0.39]  |
| genre only      |  493 | identity ~ production |  0.12 | [0.03, 0.22]  |
| genre only      |  493 | identity ~ demand     | -0    | [-0.10, 0.09] |
| genre only      |  493 | production ~ demand   | -0.04 | [-0.13, 0.05] |

The functional-only rows are the real test: the dimensions agree even with the functional/genre contrast removed. Within genre artists they do not correlate, as expected if almost all of them are real.

## Ghost-like label

2-component Gaussian mixture on the three scores. 2,656 of 5,024 names are ghost-like (p > 0.5); 6.9% have 0.1 < p < 0.9.

| set             | name                  |   n_artists | share_ghost   |
|:----------------|:----------------------|------------:|:--------------|
| known supplier  | Firefly Entertainment |          50 | 94%           |
| known supplier  | Epidemic Sound        |          40 | 0%            |
| classical major | Deutsche Grammophon   |          81 | 0%            |
| classical major | Decca                 |          81 | 0%            |
| classical major | Sony Classical        |          62 | 0%            |
| classical major | Warner Classics       |          54 | 6%            |
| genre playlists | all 5 genre playlists |         493 | 1%            |

Firefly (named in the DN investigation) is almost all ghost-like; classical majors on the same playlists are almost all real. **Epidemic Sound is classified real**: its artists have bios, links and galleries and ~9x the functional median followers per listener. The identity dimension cannot see a supplier that invests in artist profiles. Say this in the Discussion.

Deezer match rate is not a validator: tracks by ghost-like names match Deezer at almost the same rate as tracks by real names (computed in src/figures/convergence.py).

## Hand labels

`notes/hand_label_sheet.csv` has 150 artists (50 low / 30 mid / 50 high p_ghost on functional playlists, 20 genre), shuffled, without scores. Each group member labels them independently in their own column (ghost = 1 / real = 0) from the Spotify page, without looking at the scores; then run `python -m src.features.kappa` for inter-rater and model-vs-majority Cohen's kappa.
